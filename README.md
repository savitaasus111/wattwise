# WattWise: AI Electricity Bill Analyzer

## Problem
Most households get electricity bills they don't understand and don't know how to reduce them or their carbon footprint.

## Solution
WattWise lets users upload an electricity bill and uses Gemini AI to extract units, cost, and tariff. It then shows a carbon footprint estimate, a comparison with an average household, an efficiency score, and 5 personalized saving tips with estimated rupees and CO2 saved.

## Features
- Bill upload (image or PDF) with AI extraction
- Demo bill mode, no signup needed
- CO2 footprint and usage comparison chart
- Efficiency score
- Personalized saving tips
- English/Hindi toggle
- Shareable result card

## Tech Stack
Google AI Studio, Gemini API, React, Vite, Vercel
